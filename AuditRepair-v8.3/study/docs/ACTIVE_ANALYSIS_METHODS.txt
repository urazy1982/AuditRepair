ACTIVE REPAIR ANALYSIS — v8.2 RETROSPECTIVE AMENDMENT
===================================================

Purpose and status
------------------
This amendment separates policy inactivity
from the conversion of accepted corrections into expert-label savings. It uses
the fixed v8.1 factorial records. The analyses were designed after those records
were available; they are not a new preregistered efficacy
experiment. No new LLM annotations or PAC repetitions are collected.

Run from the revision directory:
    python3 -B scripts/analyze_active_repairs.py

The script reads frozen_v8_1 and, by default, compares accepted-state
preprocessing with corrected/factorial.py. It writes only active_* outputs in
results/. Passing --skip-corrected-comparison omits that extra comparison and
records the omission. Exact saved-array hash matching is mandatory for the
mechanism analysis. The script stops on a mismatch; it does not replace this
gate with a floating-point tolerance.

Populations, units and denominators
----------------------------------
The full factorial remains seven pools × three generators × two scores = 42
cells. A cell is inactive if none of its 20 preprocessing seeds certified a group
action. An active cell has at least one such seed; it is classified separately
according to whether any paired cost differs. This definition concerns the
sampled seeds, not whether a policy could ever act under other random draws.

Every cell has 20 preprocessing seeds × 10 paired PAC repetitions = 200 paired
cost comparisons. Two score variants share the same preprocessing state; they
do not create two independently accepted actions. The archived factorial has 31
accepted group actions in 31 preprocessing states, distributed over five
generator–pool combinations and therefore ten score-specific active cells.

Distributions are given under both of the following explicit scopes:

1. All prespecified preprocessing seeds: all 20 seeds and all 200 paired
   repetitions in each active cell. This is the original policy-level
   comparison, including realizations in which no repair was accepted.
2. Accepted preprocessing seeds only: the 10 PAC repetitions for every seed that
   accepted at least one action. This is a descriptive, selection-conditioned
   mechanism analysis. It is not a randomized comparison, a new policy, or an
   estimate of the gain from forcing an acceptance.

The five active generator–pool combinations have 8, 12, 2, 8 and 1 accepted states,
respectively (AG News development G0_uniform, G1, G2; AG News holdout G1, G2).
Crossing these with two scores produces 620 accepted-state paired comparisons.
The all-seed summaries of the ten active cells contain 2,000 paired comparisons.

The state classification retains all 420 pool–generator–seed states and reports
whether an accepted action changes any prediction against final same-audit DS,
and whether its incremental accuracy gain is positive, zero or negative. In
these records, all 31 accepted states make actual prediction changes and have
strictly positive gain against final same-audit DS (minimum 7 labels). There are
389 states with no accepted action and no accepted states with a zero or
negative gain against that final comparator. These empirical facts are separate
from the certificate's guarantee against the frozen reference.

Positive savings mean:
    S = total unique expert labels for same-audit DS
        - total unique expert labels for certified repair.

Both arms use the same audits, scores and calibration draws within a pair. The
same-audit baseline is DS refitted on every audit, whereas the group certificate
compares with a reference frozen immediately after the warm-up. These two label
vectors are kept distinct throughout the analysis.

Practical event thresholds
-------------------------
The frozen practical margin is 1% of pool size. Since purchases are integers,
an individual paired repetition meets the threshold if
    saving >= ceil(N/100), or saving <= -ceil(N/100) for a cost increase.

The bounds are inclusive. They are 50 labels for AG News development and 26 for
its holdout. These are repetition-level tail events; they must not be confused
with a 1% threshold on the policy's mean effect. The cost-distribution table also
reports mean savings / N and mean savings / mean baseline cost in separate,
explicitly named columns. The latter is a ratio of means, not a mean of the
individual ratios.

Distributional reporting and clustering
---------------------------------------
For each scope, report the numbers and proportions of positive, negative and
zero savings; practically important benefits and increases; min, 1st, 5th, 25th,
50th, 75th, 95th, 99th percentiles and max; and the same summaries for nonzero
effects and preprocessing-seed means. Quantiles use NumPy's default linear
interpolation and therefore need not themselves be attainable integer costs.

The seed table contains every one of the 20 seeds for each active cell, with its
mean effect and its ten-repetition event frequencies. The distribution table
also gives the number of seeds that exhibited each type of event and the
minimum, median and maximum seed-specific frequencies. A seed with positive and
negative repetitions can have a zero mean; a zero seed mean is not classified
as proof that every repetition was unchanged.

The original authoritative pointwise 95% percentile intervals for all-seed mean
effects are retained from frozen_v8_1/analysis/E2_E3_effects.csv. These resample
20 preprocessing-seed means, 4,000 times, with the shared frozen matrix per pool.
Mean values are checked against the integer paired effects. New frequency
tables receive no independent-repetition standard errors or binomial confidence
intervals. No confidence interval is computed for the accepted-only summaries.

An interval of [0, 0] remains a degenerate empirical bootstrap. It neither
establishes equivalence nor excludes unobserved algorithmic realizations. No
"rules out 1% saving" conclusion or flag is used in these amendment outputs.

Recovery of the current factorial mechanisms
-------------------------------------------
All 140 factorial checkpoints are checked for a complete 26,600-row grid,
matching frozen protocol/code hashes and paired calibration-draw hashes. The
classification and cost summaries use archived integer outcomes.

Only the 31 accepted preprocessing states require item-level recovery. For each,
the script reconstructs the archived item permutation and preprocessing using
the unchanged v8.1 implementation, data and frozen feature cache. It requires
exact saved hashes for baseline predictions, repaired predictions, restricted
oracle predictions, both score arrays, audited-item indices and the warm-up.
The complete saved group logs, shortlist and diagnostic counts must also match.
These checks precede the new mechanism calculations. Recovered scores are not
used if their hashes differ, even by an apparently small numeric amount.

The optional corrected-code comparison reruns those same 31 preprocessing
states under the publication predicate, then requires the same seven array
hashes and every recorded audit path, stopping decision, lower bound and gain
diagnostic to match. New exact-arithmetic diagnostic fields are not mistaken
for changes in the archived policy trajectory. This complements the separate
all-prefix acceptance replay; it is not a new sample.

For every accepted group, the group table records the certified population,
audit count, stopping remainder, selected grid index, exact nominal remainder
margin, actual frozen-reference gain, and gain against final same-audit DS.
It records actual changed predictions, corrections, introduced errors, changes
from one wrong label to another, and items for which final DS already agrees
with the accepted replacement. The integer nominal margin here audits the
selected bound; it is not a proof that all floating-point betting arithmetic
is exact.

All accepted remainders are retained in the item table with the original pool
row index, frozen reference, final same-audit DS, accepted prediction, gold and
the two frozen scores. Item records are joined to group records by group_id.
Multiple seeds can include the same original item; summing these rows counts
occurrences, not distinct corrected items.

For every accepted state × score × PAC repetition, the mechanism table reports
actual changed predictions, corrections, introduced errors and wrong-to-wrong
changes strictly below, equal to and strictly above the baseline threshold.
The equality region is not merged into the strict-below automatic-acceptance
region. It records both thresholds and their ranks among fixed candidate scores,
the reviewed sets, unique expert purchases, final-label differences and final
errors. Group and state identifiers link these quantities to the two gain
comparators.

Review and purchase sets are calculated independently from the recorded
thresholds, reconstructed score vector and fresh reconstruction of the archived
draw stream:
    R = {i outside the audits: U_i >= selected threshold}
    P = A union distinct(D) union R.

Both |P| and the final label error are required to match the archived outcomes.
The number of purchases removed minus the number added must equal the archived
paired saving. A raw reduction in |R| is not assumed to equal a reduction in
unique expert purchases: overlap with prepaid audits or calibration matters.

Mechanism classes
-----------------
The following mutually exclusive hierarchy classifies each accepted-state pair:

1. No actual prediction change against final same-audit DS.
2. All actual changed predictions are strictly above the baseline threshold.
   This is the sufficient structural locality condition; equality
   of thresholds, review sets, purchase sets and final labels is verified.
3. The changed predictions reach the baseline prefix, but no sampled binary
   loss changes at or below that threshold. All tested loss sequences through
   the baseline cutoff are then identical. Threshold and purchase invariance
   are verified, even though unqueried final labels can still differ.
4. Some sampled loss changes at or below the baseline cutoff, but the selected
   threshold does not change.
5. The threshold changes but the unique purchase union does not.
6. Unique expert cost decreases.
7. Unique expert cost increases.

Zero-count classes remain defined rather than being silently merged. The
structural condition is sufficient, not necessary. A label change entering the
baseline prefix is not evidence that cost must change. Classes 3–5 distinguish
three additional observed possibilities without claiming a causal decomposition
of lost savings. The classification concerns the actual sampled paired runs.

Outputs and join keys
---------------------
active_cell_classification.csv  All 42 cells; the 32/7/3 decomposition.
active_state_classification.csv  All 420 preprocessing states, including no-ops
                                 and nonpositive-gain categories if observed.
active_state_classification_summary.csv  State classes by pool and generator.
active_cost_distributions.csv  Ten active cells × two explicit scopes.
active_seed_summaries.csv      All 200 active-cell preprocessing-seed summaries.
active_repetition_effects.csv  All 2,000 active-cell paired repetitions.
active_accepted_groups.csv     All 31 accepted actions; one row per action.
active_remainder_items.csv     Item-level accepted remainders, with group_id.
active_mechanisms.csv          All 620 accepted-state paired mechanisms.

active_analysis_summary.txt    Compact numerical findings.
active_cost_distribution.png/pdf  Cluster-labelled distribution plot of the
                                 three nonzero cells, including all zero pairs.

state_id joins state and group diagnostics across scores. pair_id adds the score
and PAC repetition. accepted_group_ids joins mechanism rows to group_id (a
semicolon-separated list if a future input has multiple accepted groups). In
these archived data, each active state accepted exactly one group.

The mean score-position summaries are per accepted paired repetition. Their
corresponding sums are explicitly labelled occurrences across repetitions;
they repeatedly count the same state-level labels and are not extra independent
corrections. The plot shows nested repetitions and seed means; its point cloud
must not be used as 2,000 or 620 independent experiments.

Interpretation limits
---------------------
The analyses explain the sampled current-factorial comparisons and preserve
adverse tails. They do not identify a general causal reason that cleaning fails,
estimate effects on a new annotation panel, or prove practical equivalence.
Conditional acceptance is not a randomized treatment; restricted O1 actions
are not a cost-optimal oracle. The certificate's local reference guarantee,
the empirical final-DS gain and the expert-purchase effect are separate objects.

Verification here combines deterministic reconstruction with independent set
accounting. It is neither a separately developed implementation of the entire
cleaning algorithm nor an independent annotation campaign. Exact matching of
the corrected and frozen paths is evidence about those fixed inputs and seeds.
