STUDY RERUN ALREADY ACCEPTED

The earlier rerun.zip supplied all 320 full-scope checkpoints and 42,200 study
evaluation records: E1 12,600; E2/E3 26,600; E4 3,000. Every recorded consequential
cost/error/decision outcome matches the reference. All eight authoritative
analysis CSVs are byte-identical to the reference and to an independent
recomputation from the uploaded checkpoints.

The study artifacts are not universally bitwise identical: complete score-array
hashes can differ, and recorded thresholds differ by up to approximately
9.8e-14. The complete score vectors are not available in the rerun archive, so
their differences cannot be bounded from hashes alone. Timestamps and elapsed
times also differ. This is successful outcome reproduction on the same finite
panels and seeds, not a new independent annotation sample.

EXISTING NUMERICAL AND STATISTICAL LIMITS

The WSL main report reproduces 28 synthetic cases where a tiny positive floating
margin corresponds to a zero nominal-grid remainder margin. This is the same
known edge case as in the reference run. A separate completed audit examined all
55 accepted study groups and found none affected under the audited nominal and
represented-grid arithmetic; see ../numerical_acceptance_audit.json and the
accompanying note. The current upload does not rerun that distinct study audit.
The frozen implementation remains unchanged; the conservative arithmetic guard
is a documented recommendation for a future version.

The two existing warnings are unchanged: bisection is invalid for general
nonmonotone rejection sets, and static seed-wiring inspection is not a proof of
independence for arbitrary callers or future score-training code. Verification
does not replace a proof of the general statistical guarantees. Its simulation
intervals are individual, not simultaneous, and cover the declared fixed inputs.
Repeating the same seeds on WSL adds portability evidence, not new independent
simulation samples. The final-error target remains 90%; the conjunction with the
95% repair-stage target has an at-least-85% union-bound guarantee, subject to the
respective assumptions.
