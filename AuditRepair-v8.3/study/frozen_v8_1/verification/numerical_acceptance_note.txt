NUMERICAL ACCEPTANCE AUDIT OF THE FROZEN V8.1 STUDY

Finding
-------
None of the 55 actually accepted groups in the completed E1, E2/E3, and E4
preprocessing states was accepted solely because a zero or negative exact
nominal-grid remainder became positive in floating-point arithmetic.

Experiment    Checkpoints    Preprocessing states    Accepted groups audited    Minimum positive exact margin
E1            140            140                     11                         1/40 = 0.025
E2/E3         140            420                     31                         1/100 = 0.01
E4             40             40                     13                         1/20 = 0.05
Total         320            600                     55

The exact margins above are lower bounds on aggregate remaining gain, in label
count units. They are not observed true gains or per-item percentages.

There were zero float-positive / exact-nominal-zero cases, zero
float-positive / exact-nominal-negative cases, and zero float-positive /
exact-represented-grid-nonpositive cases. All 2,169 audit checks passed.
Runtime was 27.037 seconds. This specific numerical caveat does not require
reinterpretation of any accepted study group. Broader floating-point validity
remains outside this audit.

Coverage and recovery
---------------------
E2/E3 checkpoints preserve 2,594 group records, including the 31 acceptances.
Their integer gain histories and stored lower endpoints were read directly.

E1/E4 checkpoints preserve acceptance counts and frozen output hashes but omit
group histories. Only the 11 E1 and 13 E4 preprocessing states reporting an
acceptance were recovered with the unchanged source, protocol, and seeds.
Recovered predictions, scores, and audited item identities matched the saved
hashes. E4 additionally matched the source panel, gold, reference predictions,
and recorded audit labels. No PAC calls were allowed; no score sweep or full
study was rerun. States reporting zero acceptances needed no recovery.

All 320 checkpoint files and the relevant frozen source files were hashed
before and after the audit and remained unchanged. The original main
verification script and report were not edited. Per-group gain histories,
fractions, identities, hashes, and check counts are recorded in
numerical_acceptance_audit.json. audit_numerical_acceptance.py reproduces this
targeted audit.

Exact calculation
-----------------
Let q = 400 for the 401-point grid; j is the integer index of the selected lower
grid point; N is the tested finite-population size; and A is the integer sum
of observed gains z in {-1, 0, 1}. The nominal-grid remainder is

    R_nominal = [N*(2*j - q) - q*A] / q.

The audit locates j by exact equality with the stored np.linspace grid value,
then uses Python integer arithmetic and fractions. It also evaluates the
remainder exactly at the represented binary floating-point lower endpoint,
using Fraction.from_float. Each saved audit path reproduces its selected
lower endpoint and stopping margin, and no earlier step has a positive
floating-point acceptance margin.

The smallest margin is E2/E3, AG News 5,000, seed 14, G0_uniform, group rank 6
(zero-based): N=87, j=246, A=20, so the exact numerator is 4 and the margin is
4/400 = 0.01. The stored floating margin is 0.00999999999999801.

Conservative future implementation recommendation
------------------------------------------------
For this integer-gain setting, carry the selected lower-grid index directly
from inversion and accumulate A as an integer. Do not reconstruct either
quantity from a rounded mean in production.

    q = grid_points - 1
    nominal_num = N * (2*j - q) - q * A

The final nominal-grid sign test is then nominal_num > 0, together with t < N.
This avoids the zero-margin cancellation problem without an arbitrary epsilon.

To be conservative with respect to both the nominal rational grid and the
actual represented grid value, additionally use:

    a, b = float(grid[j]).as_integer_ratio()
    represented_num = N * (2*a - b) - b * A
    accept = (t < N and nominal_num > 0 and represented_num > 0)

This is exact positivity at the smaller of j/q and the represented grid value.
A migration that must never add an acceptance to the legacy floating rule can
also retain its old floating-margin > 0 condition. Both exact guards pass for
all 55 observed accepted groups.

Use Python integers, or separately establish overflow limits for fixed-width
integers. This rule relies on gains being exactly {-1,0,1}; do not silently
quantize arbitrary observations in [0,1]. For other observation types, use
verified interval or directed-rounding arithmetic throughout the final
expression and accept only when the lower endpoint is positive. Moving only
the final rounded result down by one representable float does not account for
rounding in earlier operations.

Scope of the recommendation
---------------------------
The proposed guards address the final remainder-sign calculation and the
nominal-grid boundary. They do not prove accuracy or conservatism of the
conditional null means, predictable stakes, log-capital updates, impossible-null
tolerances, or threshold rejection comparisons. The audit conditions on the
observed selected grid indices; exact path replay is not a proof of general
floating-point validity. No frozen implementation or reported study result
was modified as part of this audit.
